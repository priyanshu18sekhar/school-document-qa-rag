# Transport Rules and Route Timings 2026-27

Greenwood Public School, Bhubaneswar

## Charges

Transport charges are billed per term and are separate from tuition fees.

| Distance from school | Charge per term |
|----------------------|-----------------|
| Up to 5 km | 4,200 |
| 5 km to 10 km | 5,600 |
| 10 km to 15 km | 7,100 |
| Above 15 km | 8,400 |

Transport charges are not eligible for the sibling concession.

## Routes and Timings

| Route | Area covered | Morning pickup | Afternoon drop |
|-------|--------------|----------------|----------------|
| Route 1 | Chandrasekharpur, Sailashree Vihar | 6:45 am | 3:20 pm |
| Route 2 | Patia, KIIT Square | 6:55 am | 3:25 pm |
| Route 3 | Jaydev Vihar, Nayapalli | 7:05 am | 3:30 pm |
| Route 4 | Saheed Nagar, Satya Nagar | 7:15 am | 3:35 pm |
| Route 5 | Old Town, Lingaraj | 7:00 am | 3:40 pm |

Buses depart from the north gate. Students must be at their stop five minutes
before the scheduled pickup time. Drivers are instructed not to wait.

## Change of Route

A change of route or stop requires written application at least 15 days before
the start of a term. Mid-term changes are permitted only on change of
residence, supported by proof of address, and attract a one-time
administrative charge of Rs 300.

## Discontinuation

Transport may be discontinued at the end of any term with 30 days' written
notice. No refund is given for a term already commenced.

## Conduct

Students are responsible for their own belongings. The school is not liable
for items left on a bus. Repeated indiscipline on a bus may result in
withdrawal of transport facility, without refund, at the discretion of the
Principal.
