# Examination Circular: Term 2 Assessments 2026-27

Greenwood Public School, Bhubaneswar

Circular no. GPS/EXAM/2026/14, issued 22 September 2026.

## Schedule

Term 2 examinations for Classes 6 to 12 will be held from 18 November 2026 to
2 December 2026. The detailed date sheet is displayed on the notice board and
sent to parents through the school portal.

Examinations begin at 9:00 am. Students must be seated by 8:45 am. No student
is admitted to the examination hall after 9:15 am.

## Eligibility

A student must have at least 75 per cent attendance in the term to be eligible
to sit the term examination. Shortfall on medical grounds may be condoned by
the Principal on written application supported by a medical certificate.

Students with outstanding fees at the start of the examination period are not
issued an admit card until the dues are cleared.

## Marks and Weighting

The term result is computed as 80 per cent from the written examination and 20
per cent from internal assessment. Internal assessment covers class tests,
project work and practical work where applicable.

The pass mark is 33 per cent in each subject, computed on the aggregate of
written and internal components.

## Re-examination

A student who misses an examination for a medical reason may sit a
re-examination, provided a medical certificate is submitted within seven days
of the missed paper. Re-examination is held in the week beginning 15 December
2026. There is no re-examination for a student who was absent without an
approved reason.

## Malpractice

Possession of a mobile phone or any electronic device in the examination hall
is treated as malpractice, whether or not it is used. The paper is cancelled
and the matter is referred to the disciplinary committee.

## Results

Results are published on the school portal on 20 December 2026. Report cards
are issued at the parent-teacher meeting on 23 December 2026. Re-evaluation of
a paper may be requested within seven days of the result, on payment of Rs 200
per subject.
