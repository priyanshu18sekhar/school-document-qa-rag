# Staff Leave Policy 2026-27

Greenwood Public School, Bhubaneswar

Applies to all teaching and non-teaching staff on the school's rolls.

## Leave Entitlement

| Leave type | Entitlement per year | Carry forward |
|------------|----------------------|---------------|
| Casual leave | 12 days | Not permitted |
| Earned leave | 15 days | Up to 45 days |
| Sick leave | 10 days | Up to 30 days |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks | Not applicable |
| Paternity leave | 15 days | Not applicable |

Casual leave lapses at the end of the academic year and cannot be encashed.

## Application

Leave must be applied for through the staff portal at least three working days
in advance, except for sick leave, which may be applied for on the day. Sick
leave of more than two consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

Casual leave is not granted on the day immediately before or after a scheduled
holiday, except with the written approval of the Principal.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave

Maternity leave of 26 weeks is available to a female employee who has
completed 80 days of service in the preceding 12 months. It may be taken from
up to eight weeks before the expected date of delivery.

Paternity leave of 15 days must be taken within three months of the birth.

## Leave Without Pay

Leave without pay is granted only after all applicable paid leave has been
exhausted, and requires the Principal's approval. A period of leave without
pay exceeding 30 days in an academic year affects the increment due at the
end of that year.

## Encashment

Earned leave may be encashed at the time of leaving service, up to the
accumulated balance, at the basic pay applicable on the last working day.
Sick leave and casual leave are not encashable under any circumstance.
