# Fee Policy 2026-27

Greenwood Public School, Bhubaneswar

## Term Schedule

The academic year is divided into three terms. Fees are payable in advance
for each term.

| Term | Period | Due date |
|------|--------|----------|
| Term 1 | April to July | 10 April 2026 |
| Term 2 | August to November | 10 August 2026 |
| Term 3 | December to March | 10 December 2026 |

## Tuition Fees

| Class | Term 1 | Term 2 | Term 3 |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| Nursery to Class 2 | 12,500 | 12,500 | 12,500 |
| Class 3 to Class 5 | 14,800 | 14,800 | 14,800 |
| Class 6 to Class 8 | 17,200 | 17,200 | 17,200 |
| Class 9 and Class 10 | 19,500 | 19,500 | 19,500 |
| Class 11 and Class 12 (Science) | 24,000 | 24,000 | 24,000 |
| Class 11 and Class 12 (Commerce) | 21,500 | 21,500 | 21,500 |

All amounts are in Indian Rupees and are exclusive of transport charges.

## Late Payment

A late fee of Rs 500 per week, or part thereof, applies to any term fee
received after its due date. The late fee is capped at Rs 4,000 per term.

Where fees remain unpaid 45 days after the due date, the student's name is
removed from the attendance register until the outstanding amount, including
accumulated late fees, is cleared.

The late fee is waived where the delay is caused by a failure of the school's
own payment gateway, provided the parent reports the failure in writing within
seven days.

## Sibling Concession

A concession of 10 per cent on tuition fees applies to the second child and
15 per cent to the third child, when all children are enrolled in the same
academic year. The concession applies to the younger sibling's fees. It does
not apply to transport charges, examination fees or the admission fee.

## Refunds

The admission fee of Rs 25,000 is non-refundable in all circumstances.

Tuition fees are refundable on withdrawal as follows: 75 per cent if written
notice is received before the term begins, 50 per cent if received within the
first 30 days of the term, and nil thereafter.

## Payment Methods

Fees may be paid by NEFT, UPI, or demand draft drawn in favour of "Greenwood
Public School". Cash payments above Rs 10,000 are not accepted. The school does
not accept post-dated cheques.
